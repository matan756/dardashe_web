Dardashe

An app for learning spoken Arabic, built for Hebrew speakers.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Dardashe teaches everyday spoken Arabic — the local dialect people actually use in daily life in Israel — rather than Modern Standard Arabic. Short interactive lessons, designed specifically for Hebrew speakers, build practical conversation from the very first lesson: real-life vocabulary and situations, audio for every word and sentence, and immediate feedback as you practice.

Why it matters

Arabic is the native language of about 20% of Israel's citizens, yet only a small minority of Hebrew speakers can hold a conversation in it. Most available options teach Modern Standard Arabic or depend on classroom study, rather than the spoken language people hear around them every day. Dardashe closes that gap — and speaking to someone in their own language is, in itself, a gesture of recognition and respect.

How it works

  • Short lessons with clear progression, from the alphabet to everyday conversation.
  • Tap any word to see what it means, hear how it sounds, and explore its forms.
  • Words and sentences you have learned come back for review, so they stick.
  • A simple, uncluttered interface: no long instructions, no setup — just start.

Who it's for

Hebrew speakers at any level, including complete beginners — with a focus on students in universities, schools and language exchange programs.
