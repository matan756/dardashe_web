Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 June 2026

This English text is a translation provided for convenience. The binding version is the Hebrew original; in case of any discrepancy, the Hebrew version prevails.

We at Dardashe (the "App") respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. Use of the App constitutes agreement to this policy.

What information do we collect?

  • Account details – your email address, and where you sign up with Google or Apple, also the username associated with that account. This information is required in order to create an account for you and to enable sign-in.
  • Push notification token – this identifier is stored only if you have agreed to receive notifications, whose purpose is to serve as a reminder encouraging learning.
  • Learning and usage data – including completed lessons, scores, number of consecutive learning days, completed exercises and common mistakes. This data is stored in order to enable progress within the App and to provide exercises suited to the user's level of knowledge.
  • Basic technical data – such as device type and app version, for the purposes of verifying proper operation, security and performance improvement.

We do not collect:

  • audio files from users
  • location data
  • health or financial information

What do we use the information for?

  • Creating an account and signing in
  • Saving personal progress and adapting exercises to the user's level of knowledge
  • Sending reminders to maintain the learning streak (subject to your consent)
  • Improving the App's performance, detecting faults and developing new features
  • Conducting scientific research aimed at improving teaching methods and learning content. Information used for this purpose is processed without identifying details.

Sharing information with third parties

We do not sell personal information, and we do not transfer it to third parties for marketing purposes.

In order to operate the App we rely on trusted infrastructure providers, including data storage and authentication services, notification delivery, sign-in (such as Google and Apple) and email delivery.

Some of our infrastructure providers operate abroad, and therefore information may be processed or stored outside Israel. These providers comply with international security and data protection standards (such as GDPR and SOC 2).

Data retention and deletion

We retain the information for as long as your account is active. Upon deletion of the account, the personal information associated with it will be deleted.

Your rights

At any time you may request:

  • access to the information stored about you
  • correction of personal details
  • deletion of your account and all information associated with it

Requests to exercise these rights will be handled within one week.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. In the event of a material change we will publish an appropriate notice and update the "Last updated" date at the top of this document.

Contact

The App is operated by Dardashe. For any question or request, you are welcome to contact us at support@dardashe.co.il
